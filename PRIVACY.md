# Privacy policy for Shiftly

**Last updated: 7 August 2026**

Shiftly is a shift-rota app for Android. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no
analytics. This policy describes the data the app handles and the one third party that receives
anything.

Contact for any question about this policy: **tokeraaiig@gmail.com**

---

## What stays on your phone

Everything you enter into Shiftly is stored only on your device:

- your rotation — its cycle of shifts and the date you anchored it to
- your shift types, their names, colours and hours
- individual days you have corrected or swapped
- your settings, including the app's language

There is no Shiftly server. None of this is uploaded anywhere, and the developer cannot see it.
It is removed when you uninstall the app.

If you have Android's own backup enabled, Android may include this data in the backup it makes to
your Google account, so that a new phone restores your rota. That backup is between you and
Google, and is governed by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn it off in Android's
system settings.

## Permissions the app asks for, and why

| Permission | What it is used for |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Reminding you the evening before a working day. Reminders are the only notification the app sends. Declining changes nothing else. |
| Calendar (read and write) | Only if you choose to sync your rota into a calendar on your phone. Reading is needed to list the calendars you can pick from. The app writes only its own shift events and deletes only the events it created — nothing else in your calendar is read, changed or transmitted. |
| Internet and network state | Requesting ads. Nothing else in the app uses the network. |
| Advertising ID | Used by Google AdMob, below. |

All of these are optional. The app's rota, calendar and widget work with every one of them denied.

## Advertising

Shiftly shows a single banner ad at the bottom of the calendar, served by **Google AdMob**. The
app does not send AdMob anything you have typed into it — not your rota, not your shift names,
not your hours.

To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK collects device and usage information, which may include
your advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device model and
operating system version, and interactions with the ad. This is described in
[Google's Advertising privacy policy](https://business.safety.google/privacy/) and the
[AdMob partner list](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903).

Before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form where privacy law requires it
(the EU, the UK, and Switzerland). Your answer is respected: if you do not consent to
personalised advertising, personalised ads are not requested. You can reset your choice at any
time by clearing the app's data, and you can limit or reset your advertising ID in Android's
system settings under **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

## What the app never does

- No account, sign-in or email address is required or requested.
- No rota, shift or schedule data leaves your device.
- Nothing is sold or shared with data brokers.
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included.

## Children

Shiftly is meant for working adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly
collect anything from a child under 13. Ad requests are not tagged for users under the age of
consent, because the app is not aimed at them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the updated version is published
at this address. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
